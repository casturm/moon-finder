About this preview

Sunlight varies across lunar terrain. The dark side is faintly visible; lunar north and the sunlight direction follow the local sky. This preview uses an approximate lunar axis and a fixed near-side view, so precise libration and eclipses are not included.

Surface maps: NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio, LRO / LROC / LOLA. CGI Moon Kit and credits · NASA phase reference
